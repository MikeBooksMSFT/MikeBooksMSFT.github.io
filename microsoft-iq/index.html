Monday morning. Hydration sales are down twelve percent. The team has the number. But what's the story?

Sales asks marketing. Marketing calls the analyst. Someone checks with the stores. Everyone knows a piece. Nobody has the full picture.

The search begins. Sales reports, inventory, the campaign calendar. Then a conversation with the stores. Different systems. Different people. Another handoff.

The team is busy finding the story, instead of changing it. AI without business context only sees part of the picture.

Microsoft IQ. Unified intelligence for enterprise AI. Shared business understanding for people and agents.

Work IQ understands how employees work. Fabric IQ brings meaning to business data. Foundry IQ helps agents unlock the right knowledge.

Connect products, promotions, stock, and stores. Reveal business relationships, not just files. Then add work context and organizational knowledge.

The promotion ended. Stock is available. Customers still show interest. The evidence points to a hypothesis, with sources to inspect.

Ask an agent to draft the next campaign. Use that insight, available products, and the brand's approved creative guidance.

One brief. A coordinated campaign across email, social, and web. Same story. Same look. Creative work, grounded in what the business needs.

Trust comes first. Permission-aware access. Traceable sources. People review and approve before publishing. Then measure what happens.

Less searching. Shared understanding. More time for people to decide, create, and act.

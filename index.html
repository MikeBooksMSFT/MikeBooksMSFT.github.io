01 / My story

Retail is where
my story starts.

From my parents' stores to early cloud services and today's AI work, the thread through my career has stayed the same: understand people, connect ideas to outcomes, and build something useful.

THE FOUNDATION

Before cloud,
there were stores.

I grew up in my parents' retail stores. Retail wasn't an industry label to me; it was part of everyday life.

Those roots make the work personal. When I think about technology in retail, I think about the people behind the business, not just the systems inside it.

THE EARLY CLOUD YEARS

Helping businesses
take the next step.

Early in my consulting career, I worked on Microsoft's Business Productivity Online Suite, or BPOS, a predecessor to Office 365.

That chapter gave me a firsthand perspective on the shift to cloud services. It still shapes my approach: connect what's technically possible with what people can put to work.

WHERE I AM TODAY

The brands we
know and love.

Today, my work at Microsoft centers on Azure, cloud, and AI for retail and consumer goods customers.

I'm proud to work with the brands we know and love. What matters most to me is helping teams turn possibility into action—grounded in real context, not hype.

02 / Work I'm proud of

Curiosity is useful.
Building makes it real.

I like moving from conversation to artifact. Customer IQ, public prototypes, and platform strategy work are ways I take ideas far enough that other people can react to something real.

Featured project · Working prototype

Customer IQ

Connecting customer context
to better-informed action.

I'm building Customer IQ as a customer-specific intelligence platform to help account teams organize context, generate evidence-based insights, and ask grounded questions about the accounts they support.

The current prototype brings ingestion, intelligence, and interaction into a reusable foundation I can keep evolving in the open. The aim is straightforward: make customer understanding easier to use, not just easier to collect.

I've also shared a Walgreens data platform strategy that connects platform thinking with real business outcomes in retail.
